## 11. Sample Civic-Sense Educational Content

> **Why Waste Segregation Actually Matters**
> Mixed waste — wet and dry thrown together — can't be efficiently recycled or composted, even if a recycler wants to help. Once food waste soaks into paper or plastic, both become unusable.
>
> **Three simple habits:**
> 1. Keep two bins at home — wet (kitchen scraps) and dry (paper, plastic, metal).
> 2. Rinse containers before disposing — a milk carton with residue contaminates the whole batch.
> 3. Never mix batteries or e-waste with household bins — most cities have separate e-waste drop points.
>
> *Real change begins with the places we live, work and share.*
